9 September 2026
Faster, more reliable exports

PDF export works again, and you can now export as SVG

What changed

PDF export was failing for some users on the home page, Rich Editor and Invoice Generator; that is now fixed. PDF files are also smaller, and the “Generated with Write Urdu” credit now stays pinned to the bottom of every page instead of floating after short text. Word, PDF, PNG and a new SVG option are shown as clear, colour-coded icons at the top level of the export menu in both the Basic Writer and the Rich Editor, instead of hidden behind an extra click.

Why it helps

You can export your Urdu writing with confidence, get a lighter file to send or upload, and pick the right format at a glance — including SVG when you need crisp, scalable artwork.

How to use it

  1. Finish writing or formatting your document.
  2. Choose Word, PDF, PNG or SVG from the export row.
  3. Your file downloads directly — no extra menu to open first.

Try exporting from the Rich Editor →

Practise and measure your Urdu typing

A new Urdu Typing Practice and speed test

What changed

Write Urdu now has a dedicated Urdu Typing Practice tool that times you typing real Urdu passages using English-letter input, then reports your words-per-minute and accuracy.

Why it helps

You can see how fast and accurate your Urdu typing really is, warm up before serious writing, and track improvement over repeated attempts.

How to use it

  1. Open Urdu Typing Practice.

20 August 2026
Keep writing for later

Save writing to My Documents and continue on another device

What changed

Optional accounts can now save writing from the Basic Writer, Rich Editor and Urdu Keyboard. My Documents lets you open, rename, make a copy, create a share link or delete saved documents. Voice Typing can also save a transcript copy to My Documents after you sign in.

Why it helps

You can return to important writing later or continue it on another device. Normal Urdu typing and public share links still work without an account, so signing in remains optional.

How to use it

  1. Create or sign in to a free account when you want cross-device saving.
  2. Choose Save to my account from a supported editor.
  3. Open My Documents to continue, copy, rename, share or delete the saved document.

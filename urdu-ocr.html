Review before use

Which Urdu text works best? Clear printed text is the safest starting point. Simple Naskh-style Urdu usually gives the cleanest result. Nastaliq is more complex, so poetry images, newspapers, decorative fonts, handwriting, skewed photos and mixed English can need extra corrections.

How Urdu image to text works

The tool reads the writing inside your image and turns it into normal Urdu text you can select, edit, search and copy. The technology is often called Urdu OCR, but you do not need to know that term to use it.

Which font gives the best result?

Use a clear screenshot or scan with readable printed Urdu, strong contrast and enough resolution. Naskh-style printed text usually gives the cleanest result. Nastaliq can also be read, but its joined and sloping layout is more complex and may require more manual correction, especially in poetry, newspapers and heavily styled designs.

How to get a cleaner result

Crop the image around the Urdu text, keep the page straight, and avoid distant photos, blurred text, decorative backgrounds and heavy image compression. Clear dark text on a simple background is usually easier to read.

Why you should review the text

Image-to-text recognition can confuse similar letter shapes, dots, punctuation, numbers and mixed Urdu/English content. Treat the converted text as a fast editable starting point rather than a guarantee that every character is correct.

Questions

Is Urdu image to text free?

Yes. This version reads the image in your browser and does not require a paid image-to-text account.

What does Urdu OCR mean?

OCR means optical character recognition. It is the technical name for software that reads writing inside a picture and converts it into editable text.

Does my image leave the browser?

WriteUrdu does not upload the selected image or converted text to its own server for recognition. The first use may take a little longer while the text reader gets ready.

Is Naskh or Nastaliq easier to convert?

Clear Naskh-style printed Urdu usually needs fewer corrections. Nastaliq has a more complex joined and sloping layout, so it may need more review. Results can vary with font, image quality and layout.

Can I upload a PDF?

Not in this version. Capture or convert the relevant page as a PNG, JPEG or WebP image and use that image here.

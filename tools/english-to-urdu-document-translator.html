Why this preview starts with text files

The difficult question is not whether WriteUrdu can open a PDF. It is whether English-to-Urdu translation is good enough to be genuinely useful. This first slice lets us validate the translation and editing experience before adding PDF and Word extraction.

What works now

Paste English text or choose a .txt file, translate it, edit the Urdu directly, copy it, or continue editing on WriteUrdu.

What comes next

Once translation quality passes review, the same workspace will accept text-based PDF and DOCX files. Scanned images and exact source-layout preservation remain separate quality-gated work.

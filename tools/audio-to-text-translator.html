Useful for voice notes, meetings and spoken drafts

This preview is designed for short recordings such as a WhatsApp-style voice note, a spoken reminder, a meeting note or a draft you recorded on your phone.

Why language selection is explicit

Choosing Urdu or English before transcription avoids an extra language-detection step and gives the speech recognizer a clearer hint. Automatic detection can be evaluated later if evidence shows it is necessary.

Why translation comes second

Speech recognition and translation are separate jobs. The transcript stays editable so names, numbers and wording can be corrected before any translation request is sent.

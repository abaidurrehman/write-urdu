    How to read the results

    A single word can have several good translations. Part of speech helps separate noun, verb and adjective senses. Back-translations show how a suggested target word maps back into the source language, which can help you choose the closest meaning.

    Why back-translations are not synonyms

    A reverse translation can be related in meaning without being a true same-language synonym. WriteUrdu keeps those concepts separate instead of presenting a larger but less trustworthy list.

Learn Urdu typing instead of only testing it

A speed test tells you how fast you are today. Guided practice helps you become faster tomorrow. The course starts with small key groups, moves into common words and sentences, then finishes with longer passages where rhythm and accuracy matter.

CRULP-style phonetic Urdu keyboard practice

The physical-key mode follows the CRULP Urdu phonetic keyboard pattern for common QWERTY positions: for example A → ا, S → س, D → د, Q → ق and R → ر. Shift exposes important letters such as آ، ص، ڈ، غ، ڑ، ٹ، ں. The visible guide is there to help you learn the positions rather than guess them.

On a phone or tablet, choose My Urdu keyboard and use the Urdu keyboard already installed on your device. If you only need to enter a few Urdu characters rather than practise speed, use the online Urdu keyboard.

How WPM and accuracy work

WPM is calculated from correctly typed characters using the common convention of five characters per word. Accuracy compares what you typed with the target at each position. The practice also keeps a separate mistake count, so correcting an error with Backspace does not make the original mistake disappear from your session feedback.

A simple practice routine

  1. Start with a guided lesson and aim for at least 90% accuracy.
  2. Repeat difficult lessons until the key positions begin to feel automatic.
  3. Use a one-minute test for a quick daily benchmark.
  4. Move to two or five minutes when you can keep your accuracy steady.
  5. Compare yourself with your own previous best instead of chasing someone else’s number.

Private by default: lesson completion, recent scores and your streak are saved in this browser. Your practice passage is not published as a document. Clearing this browser’s site data will clear the local practice history too.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an Urdu keyboard?

No for the phonetic mode: type with English QWERTY keys and Write Urdu inserts the corresponding Urdu character. On phones you can instead choose your device’s Urdu keyboard.

What is a good Urdu typing speed?

There is no single useful target for everyone. First keep accuracy high and establish your own baseline, then look for gradual improvement in WPM without increasing mistakes.

Why does the timer start only when I type?

This keeps setup time out of your score. You can read the instructions and position your hands before the measured session begins.

Can I paste text to get a score?

No. Paste and drag-and-drop are disabled inside the practice box because the purpose is to measure actual typing.

Make a wedding invitation in 3 steps

  1. Pick your event. Nikah, Mehndi, Baraat, Walima — or Mayun, Dholki and Engagement.
  2. Choose a design. Traditional Mughal arches, Ajrak and Phulkari, or soft modern florals.
  3. Add names, date and venue. Then download the card or send it straight to WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wedding invitation maker free?

Yes. You can make, download and share as many invitations as you like without an account.

Can I make an Urdu and English invitation?

Yes. Choose Urdu, English or Urdu + English. The bilingual layout puts the event title and closing line in Urdu and the details in English.

Can I type Urdu with English letters?

Yes. Turn on “English letters → Urdu” above the name fields, type a word such as ayesha and press space. You can also type with any Urdu keyboard.

Can I make matching cards for Mehndi, Nikah, Baraat and Walima?

Yes. Add each event and keep “Use this design for all my events” on. Every card shares the design but gets colours suited to its function.

How do I send the card on WhatsApp?

Tap “Share on WhatsApp”. On most phones the card opens straight in WhatsApp; otherwise it is saved to your device so you can attach it.

Can I print the invitation?

Yes. Choose the 5×7 inch print size and use “Print or save as PDF”.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your wedding details are not published anywhere — the card only goes to the people you send it to. See Privacy for details.

About Urdu Name Art

What is the difference between Stylish Urdu Text and Urdu Name Art?

Stylish Urdu Text creates decorated wording you can copy and paste, so another app still chooses the font. Name Art creates an image that keeps your selected Urdu font.

Can I make an Urdu name DP or profile picture?

Yes. Start with the Profile square preset at 1080 × 1080, choose a Social Profile or another template pack, and check the finished name at normal phone size before sharing it.

Does this generate a new Urdu name for me?

No. This is a name-design tool, not a random-name generator. You provide the Urdu name or wording you want to design, then choose the font, template, background and output size.

Does this use the Stylish Urdu Text result?

Yes. When you open Name Art from Stylish Urdu Text, your selected wording follows in this browser. It is never placed in the page address, expires after 30 minutes and is removed after it opens.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. Type your name before choosing styles. On smaller screens, the preview appears before the full style list so you can see your design sooner.

Are uploaded images private?

Yes. Background images stay on this device while you design and are not sent to Write Urdu or an image service. See the privacy policy for details about the separate English-to-Urdu typing service.

Why use transparent PNG?

A transparent PNG removes the card background and watermark so you can place the Urdu name over another design while keeping your chosen font.

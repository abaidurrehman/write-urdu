URDU WRITERS

Community Publishing Guidelines

These guidelines explain what you can publish on Urdu Writers, why a human reviews every submission before it is public, and how to report a concern.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

In shortSubmit your own writing. A person reviews it before anyone else can read it.
  • Only submit writing you wrote, or have clear permission to publish.
  • Do not include another person's private information.
  • No harassment, hateful abuse or unsafe content.
  • No disguised advertising or bulk/automated submissions.
  • You can withdraw your own published writing at any time.
  • Readers can report a publication for review.

AUTHORSHIP & RIGHTS

Submit your own writing

Submit writing you wrote yourself, or writing you have clear permission from the author to publish. Do not copy poems, books, articles or other copyrighted work without permission. Crediting the original author does not by itself make unauthorized copying acceptable.

PRIVACY

Respect other people's privacy

Do not publish private or personal information about another person without a legitimate basis or their permission. Avoid phone numbers, home addresses, ID numbers, private messages or other sensitive personal details.

SAFETY & CONDUCT

Keep writing safe for readers

Do not submit writing that harasses or hatefully abuses a person or group, that sexually exploits or involves minors, that contains direct threats or targeted abuse, or that instructs or encourages illegal activity that could harm others.

QUALITY & SPAM

Write meaningfully, not promotionally

Urdu Writers is for meaningful writing, not disguised advertising, repeated promotional links or automated bulk submissions. Submissions that are mainly keyword stuffing or copied filler are not published.

MODERATION

How review works

Write Urdu may approve, reject or later remove writing under these guidelines. Approval means the writing is suitable for publication on the service; it is not an endorsement of the ideas or opinions expressed in it. You can withdraw your own publication at any time from My Publications, and it stops being publicly readable immediately. Readers can report a published page for a copyright, privacy, safety or spam concern using the report control on that page, or by using Contact Write Urdu.

YOUR RIGHTS

You keep your copyright

You keep the copyright in writing you own. By submitting it for publication, you give Write Urdu permission to display and distribute the approved version through the Write Urdu service until it is withdrawn or removed, subject to these guidelines and the Write Urdu terms. Write Urdu does not claim ownership of your copyright.

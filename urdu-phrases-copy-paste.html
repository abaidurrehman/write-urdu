How to write popular phrases in Urdu or Arabic

These short answers use the spellings people commonly type into search. Use the copy button when you only need the text, or send a phrase into Card Studio when you want to make an image.

Assalamualaikum in Urdu

Assalamualaikum, also searched as Assalam o Alaikum and Assalamu Alaikum, is written:

السلام علیکم

Alhamdulillah in Urdu

Alhamdulillah is commonly written in Urdu as:

الحمد للہ

Mashallah in Urdu

Mashallah, also written Masha Allah, is:

ما شاء اللہ

Inshallah in Urdu

Inshallah, also searched as In Sha Allah or InshaAllah, is written:

ان شاء اللہ

Bismillah in Arabic

The short form of Bismillah is:

بسم اللہ

First Kalma / Pehla Kalma

The First Kalma, commonly searched as Pehla Kalma or Kalma Tayyaba, is:

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَّسُولُ اللّٰهِ

Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi rajiun

This phrase is commonly copied in messages of loss or condolence:

إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ

Jumma Mubarak in Urdu

جمعہ مبارک

Eid Mubarak in Urdu

The common Eid greeting is written:

عید مبارک

Thank you in Urdu

Thank you or Shukriya in Urdu is:

Quick answers

Common questions

How do I write Assalamualaikum in Urdu?

Assalamualaikum is commonly written as السلام علیکم. The common reply is وعلیکم السلام.

How do I write Alhamdulillah in Urdu?

Alhamdulillah is commonly written as الحمد للہ.

Can I copy these phrases to Facebook or WhatsApp?

Yes. The phrases are normal Unicode text, so you can copy them into Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, messages, documents and other apps that support Urdu or Arabic text.

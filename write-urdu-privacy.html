DATA PROCESSING

Where information is processed

Drafts, Card Studio designs and static QR codes are processed in your browser. Roman Urdu transliteration suggestions and the optional whole-text conversion action use the Google transliteration service when available, so words or passages you submit for conversion may be sent to Google. Urdu web fonts are requested from Google Fonts so the selected families render consistently; the font request does not contain your card or editor text. Do not enter confidential information into a third-party service unless you accept its policy.

LOCAL DATA

How to clear data stored in your browser

Use the editor's draft-history controls, the Card Studio reset controls or the QR Generator reset controls. You can also clear site data for write-urdu.com in your browser settings. Downloaded files are outside the site's control and must be deleted from your device separately.

Accuracy & support

Review important Urdu output

Write Urdu does not promise that transliteration suggestions are a translation or always correct. Review Urdu output before publishing important content.

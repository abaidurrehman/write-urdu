DATA PROCESSING

Where information is processed

Drafts, Card Studio designs and static QR codes are processed in your browser. Roman Urdu transliteration suggestions and optional whole-text conversion use the Google transliteration service when available, so submitted words or passages may be sent to Google. Urdu web fonts are requested from Google Fonts; the font request does not contain your editor or card text.

LOCAL DATA

How to clear local data

Use the editor draft-history controls, Card Studio reset controls or QR Generator reset controls. You can also clear site data for write-urdu.com in browser settings. Downloaded files must be deleted separately from your device.

# WU-SEO-STYLISH-001 — Stylish Urdu Text SEO Acquisition

**Status:** Implemented candidate — pending CI/merge  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Owner route:** `/stylish-urdu-text-generator`

## Objective

Turn the existing Stylish Urdu Text product into WriteUrdu's second primary creation-search acquisition destination without changing its 80-style generation engine or creating programmatic exact-match name pages.

## Query ownership

The generator owns:

- stylish Urdu text generator
- Urdu stylish text generator
- Urdu name style copy paste
- stylish Urdu name
- Urdu text style copy paste

`/urdu-name-art-maker` owns exact rendered name-image intent.

## Product/search contract

- Keep `Stylish Urdu Text and Name Generator` as the product H1.
- Keep the current indexable canonical URL; do not create keyword-clone generator routes.
- Explain that copyable results are Unicode decoration, while the receiving app chooses the actual Urdu font.
- Do not guarantee rendering or character acceptance across every phone, app or game.
- Make Name Art the explicit answer for exact Nastaliq/Naskh image appearance.
- Keep input, filters and generated results before supporting SEO content on desktop and mobile.
- Keep AdSense outside the active input/filter/result task region.
- Preserve local favourites, recents, collections, sharing and Name Art handoff.

## Informational intent decision

P1.5 originally proposed one separate how-to guide. This first implementation deliberately consolidates that informational answer on the owner route under `How to make stylish Urdu text for WhatsApp, Instagram and profiles`.

Reason: the generator already owns the exact browser task, competition is materially stronger than the Card Studio wedge, and no route-level GSC evidence currently justifies splitting authority between a generator and a near-duplicate guide. A separate guide can be reconsidered only if Search Console shows a distinct informational query cluster.

## Supporting content now on the owner route

- what copyable Unicode decoration actually is;
- how to make a short stylish Urdu name/caption;
- WhatsApp, Instagram, gaming/profile and greeting use cases;
- why styles can render differently across devices/apps;
- why no universal compatibility guarantee is possible;
- when to move to Name Art for exact Urdu typography;
- Roman Urdu transliteration versus translation clarification.

## Do not do

- no thousands of programmatic name pages;
- no `/urdu-name-style/*` or platform-clone routes;
- no claim that ordinary Urdu has a complete equivalent of Latin mathematical fancy-font alphabets;
- no universal app/device compatibility claims;
- no ads inside the active generator/results region.

## Acceptance

- existing H1/canonical/schema remain intact;
- 80-style engine and result generation are untouched;
- source-visible acquisition content appears only after generated results;
- an explicit `post-workspace` ad boundary exists after results;
- copyable-text vs rendered-image distinction is visible;
- desktop/mobile P1.4 results-first acceptance remains green;
- static acquisition contract blocks compatibility overclaims and keyword-page sprawl.

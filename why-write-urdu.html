ABOUT WRITE URDU

About Write Urdu

Write Urdu helps people turn Urdu typed with English letters—or Urdu typed directly—into messages, documents and shareable designs. Core writing tools are free and do not require an account.

OUR PURPOSE

A simpler path to confident Urdu writing

Urdu speakers, learners and diaspora communities often begin on devices configured primarily for English. Write Urdu reduces that friction by providing several paths into Urdu writing rather than forcing everyone into one input method.

Start by typing Urdu with English letters, choose Urdu letters from the on-screen keyboard, or use the Rich Editor when you need headings, colours and document formatting.

MAINTENANCE

Who maintains Write Urdu

How the product is maintained: Write Urdu is independently maintained. Updates focus on reliable Urdu writing, privacy, accessibility and easier paths from first draft to finished work.

Editorial and correction policy

Guides are reviewed against the current interface and tested in a modern browser before substantial updates. Reported errors are reproduced where possible, corrected in the source and rechecked with the site’s static and browser tests.

Correction channel

Found something wrong?

Send a correction, browser issue or accessibility report through the feedback page. Include the page, browser and steps needed to reproduce the problem, and remove private writing from reports.

Send feedback →

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

What Write Urdu does not promise

Transliteration is not translation.

Roman Urdu spellings can be ambiguous, so suggestions may produce more than one reasonable Urdu word. Review important text before publishing it.

Exports depend on the browser and device.

Word, PDF, PNG, printing and native sharing can behave differently across browsers and operating systems.

Third-party services have their own policies.

Roman Urdu transliteration, web fonts and other explicitly documented services may involve external providers. The privacy page explains those boundaries.

CONTACT & CORRECTIONS

Help improve Write Urdu

Clear reports help improve accuracy and accessibility. Do not include passwords, confidential documents or private writing in a report.

How to make stylish Urdu text for WhatsApp, Instagram and profiles

  1. Start with a short Urdu name, phrase or caption.
  2. Type Urdu directly, or type it with English letters and review the Urdu result.
  3. Generate styles and filter by Popular, Minimal, Royal, Hearts, Islamic, Gaming, Social, Kashida or other categories.
  4. Copy a simpler result first and paste it into the app where you plan to use it.

Copyable text versus Urdu name art

Copyable styles stay as text, so the receiving app controls the font and may display decorations differently. Name Art creates an image with your chosen Urdu font, which is better when the exact appearance matters.

Compatibility questions

Are these real Urdu fonts?

No. The copyable results use Unicode text—the standard text format used by modern apps—with decorative characters around it. Your destination app chooses the Urdu font. For an exact Nastaliq or Naskh look, create an image in Name Art.

Why can a style look different on another phone or app?

Fonts, emoji and decorative-character support differ between phones and apps. A symbol may look different or be rejected where you paste it.

Will every style work in WhatsApp, Instagram or a game?

No universal compatibility guarantee is possible. Copy the result into the destination and check it there. If an app rejects decorative characters, choose a simpler style or use an exported Name Art image where images are supported.

Is this a translator?

No. Roman Urdu input is transliteration: it changes sounds into Urdu script. It does not translate English meaning. Direct mode keeps English and Urdu exactly as entered.

Are favourites uploaded?

No. Text, favourites and recent inputs are stored only in this browser. The optional Roman Urdu suggestion service is described in the privacy policy.

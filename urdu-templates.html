How the Urdu Template Library works

Choose a starting design and open it in Urdu Card Studio on this device. No account is needed, and your text is not sent to a server.

Choose a size for where you will share

Square designs suit general posts, landscape designs work well for wide announcements, and story designs are made for WhatsApp Status and vertical social posts. You can change the size after opening a template.

Make the design your own

  • Edit the Urdu text and optional author line.
  • Change fonts, colours, alignment, background and watermark settings.
  • Select the text on the preview to move, resize or edit it directly.
  • Download a high-resolution PNG when the card is ready.
Are these templates free to use?

Yes. The starter templates are original Write Urdu designs and are available without registration. Your finished card remains yours to edit and download.

Does choosing a template upload my text?

No. Your template, text and uploaded images stay available while you work unless you explicitly choose Publish & Share.

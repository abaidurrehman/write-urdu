# 01 — Executive assessment: UX, organic search and AI discovery

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Base:** `origin/main` @ `036ebdb` · **Type:** baseline + first implementation slices
**Labels:** **O** = observed today (production or local render, command output); **H** = hypothesis; **R** = reused from a dated earlier study in this repo.

## Bottom line

Write Urdu does not need a cosmetic rebuild. Its single largest first-screen problem today is **not in the page code**. On production, AdSense Auto Ads places in-page ads inside the homepage hero and inside the Basic Writer command bar, which pushes the editor below the fold on phones. The locally rendered page, with no ads, already meets the mobile acceptance floor.

The next-largest opportunities are already specified and partly shipped:

- the LLM-citation slices from 2026-10-12, two of which shipped on branches today;
- the Card Studio remediation (`WU-UXR-002`);
- the `www` host consolidation.

A full homepage redesign is deliberately frozen by `specs/BACKLOG.md` (rule 7, the P0.1B2 gate rule and the HOLD list). This initiative respects that freeze.

## What already exists and must be preserved

- Homepage ownership of English-to-Urdu typing (`searchTitle` / H1 "English to Urdu Typing Online"). Its metadata is frozen by `WU-SEO-CTR-001A`.
- A task-first mobile writer layout, shipped 2026-09-03/04 and repaired 2026-09-09.
- Contextual continuation with at most three actions. "Format this as a document" appears after text.
- Text handoff between tools (`js/text-handoff.js`, `workspace-handoff.js`, card/QR/social adapters).
- Recent cards on this device, never overwriting a saved card (WU-UXR-002 C).
- An explicit intent-ownership registry (`specs/WU-SEO-CTR-001A…` §4.1), central SEO config, a static schema generator and a public-language firewall. These make content changes cheap and safe.

## Critical risks to fix regardless of score

1. **In-page ads inside the active writing task (finding 2)** violate the repo's own monetisation contract and Gate B2. Fix in AdSense, then re-measure with `docs/WU-MOBILE-ADSENSE-CORE-WRITE-PAGE-EXCLUSIONS-2026-08-19.md` §"Production verification".
2. **Duplicate `www` host** keeps stale product facts in search and AI indexes.

## What this initiative did not do, and why

- **No homepage layout redesign.** `specs/BACKLOG.md` freezes it until the P0.1 post-change review. The founder chose the unfrozen slice on 2026-10-15. A gated spec is in `03-homepage-spec.md`.
- **No real AI-engine queries today.** The 2026-10-12 audit ran 16 searches. The benchmark in `04` is a repeatable test pack, not new results.
- **No GSC export today.** All search numbers are quoted from dated repo evidence with their dates.
- **No ad code changes.** Policy and repo contract both forbid hiding Google ads in code.

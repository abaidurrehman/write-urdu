What this Urdu text cleaner fixes

Urdu copied from websites, PDFs, Word documents and messaging apps can contain look-alike characters or hidden marks. These can break search, copy and paste, file exports or the way text appears in another app.

Arabic and Urdu character variants

The cleaner can safely change common Arabic forms such as ي and ك to the Urdu forms ی and ک. It can also replace older character shapes with forms that work better in current apps.

Hidden text-direction and joining marks

Invisible marks can change how Urdu, English, numbers and punctuation appear together. Some are intentional, so the cleaner shows uncertain cases for review instead of deleting them automatically.

Spacing, punctuation, kashida and numerals

Repeated spaces and obvious spaces before punctuation can be fixed automatically. Kashida may be decorative, so removing it remains your choice. Number style also changes only when you choose it.

Mixed Urdu, English and numbers

Urdu beside invoice numbers, dates, slashes, colons or hyphens can look different across apps. The cleaner highlights unusual combinations and unmatched brackets, but it does not reverse punctuation or rewrite your wording automatically.

How to use it

  1. Paste the Urdu text you want to inspect.
  2. Select Check text.
  3. Use Fix clear problems for changes that do not need a decision from you.
  4. Review yellow items before applying any optional change.
  5. Copy the result or open it in WriteUrdu for editing and export.

Questions

Does WriteUrdu upload my text?

No. Cleaning happens on this device. If you continue in another WriteUrdu editor, the text is passed only within this browser and removed from the transfer after it opens.

Is this a spelling or grammar checker?

No. It fixes character, spacing and text-direction problems. It does not rewrite your Urdu wording.

Why are some issues marked Review?

Some hidden marks, punctuation and kashida may be intentional. Removing them automatically could change how your text looks or reads, so the tool leaves those decisions to you.

Will it fix every right-to-left layout problem?

No. Apps implement mixed-direction layout around numbers and punctuation differently. The tool finds common suspicious patterns but deliberately avoids unsafe guesses.
